## Set-up

- Kernel: Linux 6.1.6, `net/netfilter` (4353 functions in the call graph).
- Ground truth: 20 netfilter CVEs present in 6.1.6 (6 tuning / 14 test); a CVE's rank is the rank of its best-ranked vulnerable function.
- LLM: `qwen3-coder:30b` (local), prompt `v3` chosen on the tuning CVEs.
- Every setup uses the same shortlist size **K = 266** = **1.1 h** of LLM time (scoring all 4353 functions would take ~18 h, 16x more).
- Fusion weight w chosen on the tuning CVEs: {'standard': 0.5, 'personalized': 0.1, 'hybrid': 0.4}.

## Threats to validity (see the numbers above)

- **LLM memorisation:** 6 of 20 fixes were written before the model's release (end of July 2025) and could be in its training data; 14 were written after it and cannot be. See the breakdown above.
- **Call graph incompleteness:** function-pointer calls are matched by field name only (over-approximation); calls hidden in macros and callbacks handed to core-kernel APIs are not captured; about 2 functions with real callers remain unconnected.
- **Small sample:** 20 CVEs (14 test); per-CVE results are reported and confidence intervals are wide.
- **Ground truth = functions changed by the fix:** some fixes change helper or init/exit functions rather than the code where the bug is triggered.
- **Tuning set size:** settings (prompt, w) were chosen on only 6 CVEs, of which 1-2 fall inside the shortlist, so the choices are weakly supported.
